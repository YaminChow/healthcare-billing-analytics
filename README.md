# 🏥 Healthcare Operations & Billing Analytics Dashboard

![Dashboard Screenshot](images/dashboard_screenshot.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides an executive-level analysis of **$255M+ in healthcare billing and operational data** across 9,300+ patient admissions. The interactive Power BI dashboard bridges financial performance with clinical capacity management to support executive decision-making.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Impact
* **Care Pathway Optimization:** Identified that **Arthritis** patients exhibit the highest Average Length of Stay (**16.0 Days**) despite lower relative gross revenue ($41.6M), highlighting an opportunity to optimize post-acute care transitions and free up acute bed capacity.
* **Facility Standardization:** Pinpointed regional revenue variance across network facilities ($478K top yield vs. lower-tier sites), establishing operational benchmarks for underperforming sites.
* **Demographic Resource Allocation:** Evaluated admission pathways across age groups, showing high emergency reliance among seniors (65+), supporting targeted geriatric triage planning.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
* **BI Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling & DAX:** Measure creation for total revenue, average billing per patient, average length of stay (LOS), and custom age group binning.
* **UI/UX Design:** Custom container hierarchy, consistent corporate palette, direct data labeling, and high scannability.
